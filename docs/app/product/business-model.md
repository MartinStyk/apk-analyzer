# Design: Apk Analyzer Pro business model

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-11-13

Repo: MartinStyk/apk-analyzer

Mode: Builder (sustainable open source)

## Problem Statement

`docs/app/product/roadmap.md` defines *what* Pro is (three pillars, a freemium mechanic, a build
order) but not the business around it: no revenue target, price, conversion assumption, cost model,
or license stance. Without those, the R0–R2 plan (~17–19 weeks) cannot be judged on whether it pays
for itself, and the order of work is chosen by feature logic alone.

## What Makes This Cool

The flagship Pro feature sells **history that already exists**. Capture runs for everyone, so the
unlock is not "start recording now" but "eight months of what changed on your phone, readable
immediately". The offer gets stronger every month a free user waits, which is unusual for a one-time
purchase and is the reason a one-time model can keep converting long after launch.

## Constraints

- **Goal:** about €400 a month net to the developer, to pay for time spent. Running costs today are
  zero; only the developer's time costs anything.
- **Audience:** ~29k monthly active users, 83k active installs (`roadmap.md:3`; the README's "2M+"
  counts lifetime downloads). Engineers, power users, security-minded people.
- **Payment:** one-time lifetime unlock only. No subscription.
- **License:** everything stays GPLv3 and public, including Pro code. Users pay on Play for the
  signed build and to support the developer. Anyone may build from source and strip the gate; that
  is accepted. No billing-free F-Droid variant is built now, because Play Billing is proprietary and
  F-Droid's main repository rejects it. Revisit on the first real request for an F-Droid build.
- **Fairness:** the free tier keeps all raw data (README promise "no paywall on the raw data") and
  the existing rule: a sample is free, the complete set is Pro.
- **Two meanings of "fail open":** *display* gates (stub labels, free samples, certificate
  changes) always fail toward showing free content. The *entitlement* check (`ProGate`) works
  like this:

  | State | Pro |
  |---|---|
  | Purchase cached locally (`EN-02`), billing unavailable or offline | Unlocked |
  | Purchase pending | Locked |
  | Refunded or revoked (seen on re-verification) | Locked again |
  | No cached purchase and billing unavailable (no Play services, sideload) | Locked |

## The math

Play prices in the EU include VAT, and Google keeps 15%. At ~20% VAT the developer nets about 71%
of the shelf price.

| Shelf price | Net per sale | Sales/month for €400 net | Share of 29k MAU buying each month |
|---|---|---|---|
| €3.99 (launch) | ~€2.83 | ~141 | ~0.49% |
| €6.99 | ~€4.95 | ~81 | ~0.28% |
| €9.99 | ~€7.08 | ~57 | ~0.20% |

A one-time unlock never renews, so this has to repeat every month. Over time, buyers come mainly
from new installs and from free users reaching a conversion moment (a certificate-change alert,
history that has built up). Industry numbers for freemium feature gates are about 2–5% lifetime
conversion (vendor blogs, low rigor), and Android payers strongly prefer lifetime purchases.

**Monthly new users needed** = sales needed ÷ lifetime conversion rate:

| Shelf price | Sales/month | New users/month at 5% | New users/month at 2% |
|---|---|---|---|
| €3.99 | ~141 | ~2,800 | ~7,000 |
| €6.99 | ~81 | ~1,600 | ~4,000 |
| €9.99 | ~57 | ~1,150 | ~2,850 |

Compare the Play Console monthly acquisitions figure against this table. If acquisitions are above
the 2% column, €400 a month is plausible even with weak conversion. If they're below the 5% column,
the price or the target has to move.

## Premises (agreed)

1. **P1: Ship recording now.** History capture goes to release builds in the next release, with no
   UI. It is implemented but gated to debug builds
   (`core/common/.../BuildConfigurationRepository.kt:19`,
   `AppHistoryRecordingEnabled -> BuildConfig.DEBUG`), so no production user has any history yet.
   Every week it stays debug-only is a week of history nobody can buy later.
2. **P2: History is the Pro v1 pillar.** Risk rules (`RI`), tracker detection (`TR`), the security
   report (`RP`) and the content backend (`CP`) are not needed to launch. They become later
   upgrades to the same unlock, at no extra charge to existing buyers. `CP-02`'s hosting cost
   leaves v1 entirely.
3. **P4: Supporter, not a lock.** The pitch is "support the open-source developer, get Pro".
   Gates stay honest (derived free sample, fail open, no ambush paywalls).
4. **Added by the user: inventory export is Pro.** It is built (`feature:app-export`, #244) and
   hidden in release (`InventoryExportEnabled -> BuildConfig.DEBUG`). Gate per design decision 7A:
   exporting one app with every raw attribute is free (also from the app detail summary sheet,
   `AppInventoryExportNavKey`), and the whole flow and preview work for everyone. Exporting more
   than one app is Pro, and the paywall opens on the Export tap. Derived columns (risk, trackers)
   will be Pro when they exist; none exist at launch. This is `BX`'s CSV/JSON export, already done.
5. **Price is tested, not decided.** Launch at €3.99 and change it based on Play Console
   conversion data.

## Approaches Considered

- **A: Gate what's built.** Billing plus gating the existing timeline and multi-app export, first
  revenue in about one release cycle. Not chosen: Pro would feel thin at launch, with no What
  Changed tab.
- **C: Supporter unlock now.** Sell a supporter purchase right away and add Pro features to it over
  time. Not chosen: it mostly sells a promise, and the conversion data would be misleading.

## Chosen Approach: hybrid, sell what's built first, full history follows

At the autoplan final gate (d6) the user switched from B (full history first, D6/D27) to a hybrid
order. Pro goes on sale in N+1 with the surfaces already built: the per-app timeline and multi-app
export. The rest of the history experience (What Changed, the cross-app log, alerts) ships in N+2
as a free update for buyers. Revenue and price data arrive about one release cycle earlier, but Pro
is thinner at launch, so N+1 conversion understates the full product.

**Release N (now, free): recording on.** Turn on `AppHistoryRecordingEnabled` for release builds,
phone and TV (both manifests already declare `AppHistoryBackupAgent`). Nothing changes on screen,
but real background work starts for every user. All of it is already built and gated only by that
flag (`AppHistoryCaptureSchedulerImpl`): a full `reconcileAll` sweep shortly after process start,
a capture on every package broadcast, and a weekly WorkManager reconciliation. This is `HI-11`
background detection, so it ships in release N, not N+1.

Gates before release N goes out:
- **`OQ-07` measurement** on a ~300-app device: time and battery cost of the first sweep, and
  database size after the baseline plus projected growth. **Ceiling:** projected 12-month store
  ≤ 50 MB on that device. Above that, `HI-04` pruning ships in release N.
- **`HI-20` capture integrity** checked on at least two real devices (phone and TV): no snapshot
  diffed as complete when a section failed.
- **C1 privacy policy** rewritten and published, after checking what Firebase Analytics,
  Performance and Crashlytics actually collect today. The Play Console **Data safety form** is
  updated to match. Billing is described as upcoming; a second policy and Data safety revision ships
  with N+1, when billing goes live.
- **Disclosure (D18):** the release N notes say the app now keeps an on-device history of app
  changes for an upcoming feature, and that it's included in Android backup. Together with the C1
  policy, that's the disclosure. No UI in release N.

Production monitoring and rollback:
- **Signals:** existing Firebase Performance traces `app_history_reconcile` and
  `app_history_capture` (captured/failed/degraded counts), Crashlytics crash-free users, and Play
  vitals ANR rate. No new production telemetry, and in particular no database-size metric. (The
  policy is still rewritten under C1, because it is stale for other reasons.)
- **Rollout:** Play staged rollout at 10% → 50% → 100%, one step every few days.
- **Halt if any of these happen (D23):** ANR rate ≥ 0.40%; crash-free users drop more than 0.2
  points against the previous release; or failed captures exceed 5%, measured as
  `failed_count / appCount` on `app_history_reconcile` (d13). Judge each step only after ≥ 48 hours and ≥ 1,000 sessions on it.
- **Rollback is a hotfix release** with recording off. History already stored is kept, and capture
  resumes from it when recording comes back on.

**Release N+1 (Pro launch, hybrid d6): items 1, 3, 6, 8 and 9 below, plus the Settings Pro row.**
The Pro surfaces at launch are the per-app timeline (`HI-08`/`HI-15`, entries locked through
`HistoryAccessPolicy`; certificate changes stay free) and multi-app export (7A). `HI-18` ships in
N+1 because the timeline's free/locked split depends on it. Stub entry points exist only on the
timeline and at Export; the sheet is also reachable from Settings.

**Release N+2 (free update for buyers): items 2, 4, 5 and 7 below** (`HI-05`/`HI-21` first run and
pre-history, What Changed with its sample, stubs and badge, the `HI-07` log, `HI-12`/`HI-13`
alerts). The paywall's unlock lines name only what is live in that release.

Build order across both releases:
1. `EN-01`–`EN-06`: Play Billing one-time product, purchase state and restore, one `ProGate`, the
   reusable "sample free / complete Pro" UI, paywall, purchase-funnel analytics. Funnel and purchase
   events carry the app's own install date as a cohort, so post-sale installs can be separated from
   launch-week buyers (Play Console reports by purchase date, not install date). A reinstall resets
   the install date and counts as a new install; that small bias is accepted. Check Play's sale rules
   (minimum discount and duration) before fixing the €2.99 example. (Play Billing
   dependency signed off by Martin on 2026-11-13, `OQ-02`.)
2. `HI-05`, `HI-21`: first-run baseline and pre-history seeding. This is the first impression for
   every existing user.
3. `HI-18`: significance rules. One rule set decides what alerts, what gets highlighted and what is
   free vs. locked.
4. `HI-14`, `HI-06`, `HI-19`, `HI-09`: What Changed tab, free sample card, locked stubs with the
   unlock path, new-changes badge.
5. `HI-07` cross-app change log: one chronological feed of every change across all apps,
   filterable by change type. It's the surface a buyer reads after unlocking stubs in What Changed.
6. `HI-08` and `HI-15` (already built) moved behind `ProGate` instead of the debug flag.
7. `HI-12`, `HI-13`: notifications and their settings, built on the background detection that
   shipped in release N. Certificate changes notify everyone for free, everything else is Pro, and
   all of it is off until the user opts in.

8. Inventory export: `InventoryExportEnabled` turned on for release; exporting more than one app asks for Pro on the Export tap (7A). Single-app export with every raw attribute is free. No derived columns at launch.
**N+1 rollout (D24):** staged at 20% → 100%. Halt if purchase-acknowledgement failures or a crash
spike appear in the first 48 hours. The Pro product must be active in Play Console before the 20%
step.

**Purchase acknowledgement (required by `EN-01`/`EN-02`):** Play refunds any purchase that isn't
acknowledged within 3 days. The implementation owner must prove acknowledgement happens on the
first successful verification and is retried on every launch until it succeeds.

**TV (D22):** out of scope for Pro v1. TV records history from release N but gets no paywall, gate
or Pro UI in N+1. TV has no history or export screens today, so a phone purchase unlocking the
shared entitlement on TV has no visible effect.

9. **C4 launch-week sale (D19):** a Play scheduled promotional price (e.g. €2.99 for 7 days) open to
   everyone, announced in the N+1 release notes. The paywall shows whatever price Play returns and
   carries no "launch week" label, so no code knows the sale window.

"Full history experience" means exactly this, complete in N+2: the What Changed tab with digest,
sample and stubs, the cross-app log, per-app timelines with change detail, and alerts. N+1 ships
only the per-app timelines from that list.

**Later, same unlock, no re-charge (d5: the promise names these features, nothing open-ended):**
the N+2 history features, then `RI` → `TR` → `RP` → the rest of `BX`, then `OP`. Each one is a
reason for a free user to buy. Features outside this list may be sold separately.

## Success Criteria

- Release N reaches 100% rollout without tripping any halt condition, and the pre-release
  measurement stays within the 50 MB 12-month ceiling.
- Pro launches in N+1 with the per-app timeline and multi-app export; N+2 completes the full
  history experience for buyers at no extra charge.
- Funnel analytics (`EN-06`) report gate impressions → paywall views → purchases from day one.
- 90 days after the launch-week sale ends: a measured conversion rate on installs that arrived
  after the sale, and a price decision based on it. Launch-week buyers skew toward the most
  motivated long-time users, so they're reported separately and excluded from the price decision.
- Target: €400 a month net, sustained, within 12 months of the Pro launch.

## Distribution Plan

The existing pipeline covers it: an annotated tag builds both apps and uploads them to the Play
beta tracks, then they're promoted to production (`release-app` skill). Release N goes out as a
staged rollout. The Pro product is set up in Play Console as a one-time in-app product. Nobody
ships an F-Droid build. Self-builders get Play Billing code that can't complete a purchase, and
they're free to remove the gate.

### Entry points (1B) and frequency (3B)

```
  Already owned/restored| Gates are absent; "Restore purchase" in the sheet shows "Pro is active"
  Refund relock         | Silent: locks return with no message (2B, user decision)
```

### Return after purchase (3A)
The sheet closes. A tapped stub expands in place to its full diff. From Export, the export the user started continues. A one-time line "Thanks for supporting Apk Analyzer" appears at the top of the origin screen.

### ProLock component (5A)
New in `core:design-system:phone` (via the `create-compose-component` skill): a `Tag`-style chip with a lock icon from `ApkAnalyzerIcons` and the label "Pro", colored `primaryContainer`/`onPrimaryContainer` from `AppTheme`, with light and dark previews. Its contentDescription is "Part of Pro". It's used on stubs, locked rows, derived export columns and alert toggles. It replaces the 🔒 emoji in `app-history.md`, and is **never** used on pre-history rows.

### Accessibility and sizing (6A)
By user decision, the app relies on Material component defaults. No extra TalkBack phrasing, font-scale or tablet-width rules are specified (see the design debt below).

### Autoplan DX additions (developer-facing contract, P1/P5)

- **Entitlement contract.** New `core:billing` module with `EntitlementRepository` (interface + internal Impl, Hilt `@Binds`, `@Singleton`): `observe(): Flow<Entitlement>`, where `sealed interface Entitlement { Unlocked(source: Cached | Verified); Locked(reason: NotPurchased | Pending | Revoked | BillingUnavailable) }`. Purchase, restore and acknowledge return `Result` of a `sealed interface BillingError` mapped from Play `BillingResponseCode`. ProGate and the paywall state both derive from `Entitlement` (one predicate). Kept out of `ConfigurationRepository`, which stays for static build config. Verify: core unit tests map every response code and every entitlement state.
- **Play error mapping.** Retryable codes (SERVICE_DISCONNECTED, NETWORK_ERROR, SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE): "Couldn't reach Google Play. Try again." ITEM_UNAVAILABLE / BILLING_UNAVAILABLE: the "Billing unavailable" state, no retry. DEVELOPER_ERROR: Crashlytics non-fatal plus the generic line. Every failure logs an EN-06 event with the code.
- **Acknowledgement.** On purchase, enqueue a one-off WorkManager job with backoff in addition to the per-launch retry. Before each retry, reconcile with `queryPurchasesAsync` (`isAcknowledged`); "already acknowledged" counts as success. Each failure logs an EN-06 event with the code; this is the metric D24's halt reads.
- **Relock and pending.** A relock logs a `pro_revoked` EN-06 event and `Logger.d` (the UI stays silent per 2B). A sheet closed in the pending state from Export shows one line on the export screen: "Purchase pending. Export again once it completes."
- **PaywallSource and the sheet's home.** A new feature `feature:pro` (created with `create-feature-module`): `api` holds the sheet NavKey and `enum class PaywallSource { Stub, Timeline, Log, Alerts, Export, Settings }`, used by the NavKey and by EN-06; `phone` holds the sheet screen and its ViewModel; `core:billing` holds only the repository. Before building the sheet, check whether a bottom-sheet `SceneStrategy` exists in the Navigation 3 setup; if not, budget for one.
- **Debug entitlement override.** Debug builds bind a fake billing implementation whose state can be forced (owned, not owned, pending, error, unavailable) so contributors can reach every Pro surface and every paywall state; release builds always bind real Play Billing. `AppHistoryDisplayEnabled` and `InventoryExportEnabled` are deleted, which makes the timeline and the export entry always visible (they gate visibility today, not entitlement: `AppsViewModel`, `AppDetailViewModel`, `AppDetailState`). ProGate applies at a finer level: on the Export tap when more than one app is selected, and per timeline entry through the history access policy. Documented in README "Common tasks".
- **Testing Pro.** `core/billing/AGENTS.md` gets a "Testing Pro" section: Play license testers, internal testing track, test cards (always declines, slow for pending), refunds from Play Console.
- **Release runbook.** The `release-app` skill gains the D23 halt checks for release N and, for N+1, D24's halt checks, "Pro product active before 20%" and "schedule the launch-week sale".
- **Self-builders.** One README line: a self-built release is a separate install (debug keystore, same application ID), and Play purchases can't complete there.
- **App Functions.** Today's functions (`findApps`, `getAppDetail`, `getAppPermissions`, `getAppCertificates`) return raw data and stay ungated. Any future history or export App Function checks ProGate inside the function body and returns a typed "Pro required" error the agent can relay.

### Autoplan eng additions (architecture, data safety, tests; P5/P1)

- **One history access policy.** One injected class, `HistoryAccessPolicy`, in a module allowed to depend on both `core:app-history` and `core:billing` (a `feature:app-history:domain`, or a new core module if a second feature needs it). It returns `EntryAccess { Visible, Locked, PreHistory }` from Entitlement × HI-18 × pre-history. The stubs, the cross-app log, the per-app timeline, the HI-09 badge and the DT4 counts all read it; none writes its own AND. `core:app-history` never depends on `core:billing`.
- **Return after purchase without screen results.** Each origin ViewModel keeps its own pending intent in state (`pendingExport`, `expandAfterUnlock = entryId`, `focusAlertToggle`) and acts on it when `Entitlement` turns `Unlocked`. No result is passed back through navigation.
- **History schema is a production contract from release N.** Release-N gate: the shipped Room schema JSON is committed. From N+1, every schema bump has a `MigrationTestHelper` test in `core:app-history`; `fallbackToDestructiveMigration` is forbidden there. The backup restore merger rejects (or migrates) a staged database with a newer schema version than the app knows.
- **Entitlement cache stays out of Auto Backup.** Add `data_extraction_rules.xml` (API 31+) and a `fullBackupContent` file (older APIs) that exclude the entitlement store in both manifests. Same files are where D25's history-size fix lands. A cache found without a local verification is treated as unverified until the first successful `queryPurchasesAsync`.
- **Relock rule.** Relock only on `BillingResponseCode.OK` with no matching purchase on two consecutive launches; any error code keeps the cached state. Acknowledge only purchases in `PURCHASED` state, never `PENDING`. The cache never expires: a refunded buyer who blocks Play keeps Pro; accepted under "sell convenience".
- **Release-N halt signals made readable.** The failed-capture ratio is `failed_count / appCount` on `app_history_reconcile`, not the per-app `app_history_capture` trace. The exact Firebase console filter goes in the `release-app` runbook. OQ-07 sweep time and battery figures are informational, not halt thresholds.
- **Real billing is tested only on Play.** "Testing Pro" states that real purchase, acknowledgement and refund work only in a Play-signed build from the internal track with license testers; the D24 runbook has a smoke checklist for it. The acknowledgement worker resolves the same Hilt binding as the app.
- **Alert channel privacy.** The certificate and change alert channel uses `VISIBILITY_PRIVATE`, so app names don't show on the lock screen.
- **Install-time caveats.** The install-date cohort uses `firstInstallTime`, which also resets on a restore to a new device; note the bias in the cohort analysis. The restore merger must not create duplicate HI-21 pre-history rows when the new device seeds its own.
- **Alert latency (decided at the final gate, d12: daily).** Package broadcasts are received only while the process lives (`PackageChangesObserverImpl` registers at runtime); otherwise the weekly worker (battery-not-low, storage-not-low) is the only trigger, so an alert can be up to about 7 days late. Decision: a daily worker that uses `PackageManager.getChangedPackages(sequenceNumber)` so each run is cheap, with OQ-07 measuring the battery cost; alert copy never promises "instant". Ships with alerts in N+2.
